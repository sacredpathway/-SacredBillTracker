Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 23, 2026

Sacred Bill Tracker (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides a mobile app that helps users track bills and due dates.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the app’s core features:

  • Account information: If you sign in with Apple, we receive the authentication information necessary to create and secure your account.
  • User content: Bills and related information you enter, such as bill name, amount, due date, category, recurrence, auto-pay status, and notes.

How We Use Information

We use your information only to:

  • Authenticate your account
  • Store and sync your bill data
  • Provide app functionality
  • Maintain security and reliability

Data Storage

Your account and bill data may be stored using Supabase, our backend service provider, so the app can securely save and sync your information across sessions.

Tracking and Analytics

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with service providers as needed to operate the app, such as authentication and database hosting.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide the app and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

Your Choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app at any time (Settings → Delete Account).
  • You can request deletion of your stored data by contacting us.

Children’s Privacy

Sacred Bill Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact
