Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

Sacred Bill Tracker (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides an Android app that helps users track bills, due dates, income, focus tasks, schedule blocks, and to-dos. This policy describes the Android version of Sacred Bill Tracker, package com.sacredbilltracker.app.

Information We Collect

The Android app does not require an account, email address, phone number, bank connection, or sign-in. We do not collect bill, income, focus, schedule, to-do, or export data from the Android app.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your Android device when you choose to enter it:

  • Bill data: bill name, amount, due date, category, recurrence, auto-pay status, notes, and paid status.
  • Income data: income source, amount, date, type, and notes.
  • Sacred Focus data: focus tasks, to-dos, repeat settings, priorities, and completion status.
  • Schedule data: schedule block titles, times, repeat settings, and reminder settings.
  • Budget Together preparation: local sharing preferences saved on the device. The Android app does not send partner invitations in version 1.0.9.

How We Use Information

The Android app uses locally stored information only to provide app features, including:

  • Showing bills, due dates, totals, reports, and calendar views.
  • Showing weekly income summaries.
  • Creating local reminders and Sacred Focus tasks.
  • Creating CPA export files when you choose to export.

CPA Exports

CPA exports are created locally on your device as ZIP/CSV files. The app opens the Android share sheet only when you choose to share an export. You control where the exported file is sent or saved.

Subscriptions and Billing

Optional Pro and Focus Plus subscriptions are handled by Google Play Billing. Google Play processes purchases, renewals, cancellations, and subscription management. We do not receive or store your payment card information in the Android app.

Cloud Sync Status

Cloud Sync is not active in Android version 1.0.9 unless a secure backend is configured in a future release. This Android build does not include backend credentials, and it does not send bill or income data to a sync server.

Network Access

Core bill tracking works locally. Internet access may be used for Google Play Billing and any system services required by Android or Google Play. Sacred Bill Tracker does not use network access to upload your local bill, income, focus, schedule, to-do, or CPA export data to us.

Tracking and Analytics

We do not use third-party advertising, and the Android app does not include analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Because Android version 1.0.9 does not collect your local app data, we do not share that local app data with third parties. Subscription purchases are processed by Google Play under Google’s own terms and policies.

Data Retention

Bill, income, focus, schedule, to-do, partner-preparation, and export data remain on your device until you delete them, uninstall the app, clear app storage, or delete exported files yourself.

Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete bills, income entries, focus tasks, and schedule items inside the app where those controls are available.
  • You can delete local Android app data by uninstalling Sacred Bill Tracker or clearing the app’s storage in Android system settings.
  • You can delete CPA export files from the location where you saved or shared them.
  • You can cancel or manage subscriptions in Google Play.

Device Backup

If Android device backup is enabled on your device, Android or Google may back up app data according to your device and Google account settings. This backup behavior is controlled by Android and Google, not by Sacred Bill Tracker.

Financial Features

Sacred Bill Tracker is a personal finance organizer. It does not connect to bank accounts, move money, pay bills, provide loans, offer credit, or provide financial, legal, accounting, or tax advice.

Children’s Privacy

Sacred Bill Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
